# Scent Blend — session question bank

Session: 30 October 2026. Adapted from the supplied Russian business check-up map and local business context.

These are discovery questions, not assertions about current operations.

## 01  Direction & model

### Business lines & outcomes

- Which lines are active: space scenting, private label, distribution, brand experiences, B2C shop?
- What should change in 12 months? Which three outcomes define success, and from what baseline?

### Customers & value

- Who buys, who pays, and who uses each offer? What problem do they pay us to solve?
- What counts as a customer: legal entity, brand, group, contact or site? How are these linked?

### Strategic choices

- Which segments and offers deserve focus? What will we stop, and what constraint limits growth?

## 02  People & decisions

### Roles & ownership

- Who owns sales, scent design, sourcing, installation, service, finance and the shop? Employees or partners?
- Where does the owner spend time? Which decisions require approval, and who is the backup?

### Capacity & knowledge

- What workload can each role handle? Which skills, incentives or missing instructions create bottlenecks?
- How are people hired and trained? Which questions repeatedly come back to the owner?

### Handoffs & accountability

- At each handoff, who accepts the work, what must be complete, and where is responsibility recorded?

## 03  Demand & sales

### Acquisition & qualification

- Which channels bring qualified B2B enquiries and B2C orders? Who handles messages and follow-ups?
- What qualifies a lead: segment, site size, need, budget, decision-maker or timing? Where is it recorded?

### Enquiry → agreement

- Walk through a recent sale: enquiry, analysis, scent proposal, trial if any, quote and signed agreement.
- Who owns each stage? What are conversion, elapsed time and loss reasons? Is the 24-hour reply promise met?

### Renewal & expansion

- How are renewals, cancellations and additional sites identified? Who owns retention and follow-up?
- Which opportunities are signed, proposed or only possible? What is the next action and decision date?

## 04  Offers & production

### Catalogue & commercial terms

- What is a product, SKU, scent recipe, service package and custom project? Which versions must be tracked?
- What is sold, rented or loaned? What do recurring fees include: equipment, refills, visits or replacement?

### Custom work & suppliers

- Trace a private-label order: brief, sample approval, recipe, packaging, purchasing, production and delivery.
- Who owns recipes and approvals? What are minimum orders, lead times, supplier dependencies and rework causes?

### Quality & economics

- How are batches, quality checks and supporting product documents linked? Who resolves a failed batch?
- What are price, direct cost and contribution by offer? How are satisfaction and repeat purchases measured?

## 05  Delivery & service

### Site & installed equipment

- Trace a new site from signed agreement to installation and acceptance. Who schedules and performs the work?
- Which device is at which site, with what scent and settings? Who owns it, and how is its history recorded?

### Stock & replenishment

- How are refills and visits triggered? Who plans routes, stock, purchasing and supplier follow-up?
- What happens when stock runs out, a device fails, a site moves or a contract ends?

### Support & fulfilment

- Trace one complaint: channel, ticket, priority, owner, resolution, cost and customer confirmation.
- Trace one shop order: payment, stock allocation, shipment, delivery, return or refund. Which systems exchange data?

## 06  Money & evidence

### Contract → invoice → cash

- Who invoices each service period or milestone? How are invoices linked to contracts, sites and payments?
- How are deposits, partial payments, overdue invoices, VAT and CZK/EUR amounts distinguished?

### Revenue, cost & cash

- What is recurring contracted revenue versus one-off sales and pipeline? How are annual fees allocated by period?
- Which records explain margin and cash: invoices, inventory, delivery costs, unpaid bills, other accounts and owner funding?

### Reconciliation gaps

- Can we connect Stripe orders, fees, refunds and payouts? A bank payout alone does not identify a buyer.
- Reconcile AURES/Mototechna scope, Clever/CodeClever identity and invoice-period differences before combining records.

## 07  Data & next actions

### Entities & relationships

- Validate: entity → sites → installed devices; agreement → service lines; order → items; invoice ↔ payments.
- For every object: definition, unique ID, owner, key fields, statuses, source of truth and links. What is missing?

### Process & system map

- For each workflow: trigger → steps → owner → handoff → output. Capture rules, exceptions, tools and delays.
- Where are duplicate entries or manual transfers? Which system is authoritative when records disagree?

### Close with decisions

- Select three problems by impact and effort. Assign an owner, next action, due date and success measure.
- Separate confirmed facts, hypotheses and unknowns. For each unknown, name the evidence and who will obtain it.
