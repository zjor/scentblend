# CRM configuration specification

Status: discovery draft; the current workspace configuration has not been inspected.

This document will be the versioned specification for the approved CRM configuration. Distinguish proposed changes from verified, applied settings.

## What to capture

For each entity, record its business definition, identifier, fields, field types, required fields, allowed values, relationships and validation rules. Map business fields to actual CRM field IDs. Record pipeline stages, automations, the configuration owner and applied change dates.

Start by validating customers, contacts, sites, opportunities, orders and agreements. These are candidate business entities; their mapping to CRM objects still needs confirmation.

The [customer card](../../customers/customer-card.md) holds the business requirements. Actual customer and order records remain in the CRM.
